## 📌 Overview

**FlowPay** is a proposed application that helps users **track, monitor, and control their BNPL (Buy Now, Pay Later) balances** from services like **Tabby and Tamara**. It provides a smart dashboard, payment reminders, and plan comparison tools, allowing users to stay in control of their finances and make better payment decisions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

With the growing use of **Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)** services like Tabby and Tamara, users often struggle to:
- ❌ Manage multiple installment plans across different providers
- ❌ Track upcoming payments and due dates
- ❌ Maintain control over their outstanding balances
- ❌ Avoid late fees and penalties
- ❌ Budget effectively and reduce financial stress

**Survey Finding:** 72.7% of respondents do not record their expenses, and 51.5% report their income does not last the entire month.

---

## 💡 Solution

FlowPay provides:
- ✅ **Smart Dashboard** - View all BNPL plans in one place (paid vs. outstanding installments)
- ✅ **Payment Reminders** - Notifications for upcoming due dates to avoid late fees
- ✅ **Spending Reports** - Monthly and yearly summaries to analyze spending habits
- ✅ **Plan Comparison** - Evaluate BNPL offers for better decision-making
- ✅ **Balance Tracking** - Real-time view of total outstanding balances
